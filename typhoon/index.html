Scenario 1 Inari on the dry day

Straight to Osaka

Off the Shinkansen and straight on to Osaka for a full city day, Umeda Sky at night included. Dry Thursday takes Fushimi Inari at sunrise, then Nara, then Higashiyama and Gion. Arashiyama goes to Friday morning, the part of Kyoto that copes best with rain.

Why
Osaka almost complete. Inari and Nara's open lawns, the two things rain hurts most, get the dry day.
What it costs
Nigatsu-dō. Thursday is the biggest day on foot of the trip: a mountain, then Nara, then Higashiyama.

Inari or the bamboo grove?

Inari is the bigger highlight. It is two to three hours up a mountain through thousands of gates, with a view at Yotsutsuji crossing, and it suffers most in rain: slippery steps and no view. The bamboo grove is ten to fifteen minutes on a flat path. Arashiyama is worth it as a whole morning, with Tenryū-ji and the river, and all of it holds up in rain. So both scenarios give Inari the dry Thursday and Arashiyama the wet Friday.

If you stay after all

Wednesday and Thursday play out the same either way. Friday becomes Arashiyama and Nishiki with a free afternoon, or Osaka's arcades. Saturday keeps Nozomi 16 at 12:01 as booked, with Inari already done.
