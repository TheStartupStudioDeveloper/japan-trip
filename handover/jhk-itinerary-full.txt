### How-to 4
How-to 4. Tax-free shopping Take the physical passports. The shop scans the entry sticker. A photo is not accepted. Spend ¥5,000 or more before tax in one store on the same day. Food and other goods can be added together to reach it. Look for the Tax-Free sign, or the tax-free counter in a department store. Prices already include Japan's tax. Tax-free means it isn't charged: 10% on most goods and alcohol, 8% on take-away food and soft drinks. That is about 9% and 7% off the shelf price. Restaurant meals are never tax-free. Department stores refund at a separate counter, less about 1.5%. Food, drinks and cosmetics are sealed in a bag. Keep it sealed until you leave Japan. Tax-free QR, at home: in Visit Japan Web , personal details, turn on "Use tax-free QR code" and scan both passports with the phone camera. Tax-free QR, at Narita : immigration sticks a landing permission sticker with a QR in each passport. Check you both get one. Then in Visit Japan Web open the tax-free section, scan each sticker, and the QR is ready. Show it live at the till; carry passports anyway. Back in Australia: AUD 900 of goods, 2.25 L of alcohol and 25 cigarettes each. Declare all food and wood. Undeclared items risk a fine of about AUD 626. This at-the-till system runs to 31 October, so it covers the trip. It changes on 1 November.

Getting around, Mon 19 from Hase to home A la Mode to Hase-dera , 7 minutes: walk inland up the main road (Hase-dōri) to the traffic light by Hase Station, turn left, and the gate is straight ahead at the end of the road. ¥400, open to 17:00. Go up the steps to the lookout terrace for the sea view. Hase-dera to the Great Buddha, 7 minutes: back down to the main road, turn left (inland) and walk up it. Kōtoku-in is on the left. ¥300, last entry 16:45. Optional Yuigahama : from the Great Buddha walk back down the main road past Hase Station to the sea, about 12 minutes. Back to Hase Station is 5 minutes. Hase to Kamakura : Enoden from Hase Station, platform for Kamakura (鎌倉方面), 3 stops, about 5 minutes. Kamakura is the end of the line. Kamakura Station to lunch: walk through to the JR east exit. Komachi-dōri starts at the red torii arch on the left of the plaza. Shirasu-don at Wasai Yakura , 2-7-28 Komachi, 2F, open 11:00 to 21:00, about halfway up on the left side. Komachi-dōri to Tsurugaoka Hachimangū : walk to the far end of Komachi-dōri (about 8 minutes), turn right, and the shrine grounds are ahead. Up the big stone steps to the main hall. Free. Hachimangū back to the station: down the central raised path (Dankazura) on Wakamiya-ōji, about 10 minutes, straight to Kamakura Station east exit. Kamakura to Yokohama : JR Yokosuka line, platform for Yokohama and Tokyo (横浜・東京方面), about 25 minutes. At Yokohama , change to the JR Negishi line (light blue, 根岸線) towards Ōfuna or Isogo, one stop to Sakuragichō . Air Cabin : out of Sakuragichō east exit, the cable car station is right there on the plaza. ¥1,000 one way, about 5 minutes, lands at Unga Park beside Cosmo World . If it is stopped for wind, walk 10 minutes along the Kishamichi promenade instead. Cosmo World : Diving Coaster Vanish and the Cosmo Clock 21 ferris wheel. Buy tickets at the machines, cash only. Lockers by the coaster. Harbour walk to Chinatown , about 30 minutes: east from Cosmo World along the Kishamichi promenade to the Red Brick Warehouses , then along the water past Ōsanbashi Pier into Yamashita Park . Leave the park at its middle and walk inland about 5 minutes into Chinatown . Kyūryū SPA ( Kirin Kowloon ): Yamashitachō 148, 2F, on a pedestrian lane inside Chinatown . Open to 22:00. Ask for the 60-minute foot course with the foot bath. Backup: Kaisokukan , open to 23:00. Home: Motomachi-Chūkagai Station (Minatomirai line) is at the Yamashita Park end of Chinatown . Any train towards Shibuya (渋谷方面), express (急行) or limited express (特急) is fastest, about 40 minutes. If it carries on past Shibuya , get off at Shibuya . Shibuya to the hotel: the Tōyoko line platforms are deep underground. Follow signs up and out towards Miyashita Park , about 7 to 10 minutes to the hotel.
