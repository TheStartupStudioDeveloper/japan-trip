    Watching

    How much rain is that

    Under 1 mmLikely dry, a few spits at most.
    1 to 5 mmLight rain. Umbrella, carry on.
    5 to 15 mmSteady rain. Wet shoes, views gone.
    Over 15 mmHeavy. Move outdoor plans.

    Chance and amount

    The percentage is the chance of at least 1 mm of rain in that time. The millimetres are the single most likely amount. So 40% with under 1 mm means probably dry, with a fair chance of a light shower.

    How sure is each day

    FirmWithin 5 days. Plan on it.
    Fair6 to 10 days. The pattern is right, the timing of rain can shift a day.
    Outlook11 days or more. A trend from long-range models, not a forecast. Don't move anything yet.

    Which source wins: far out, every source counts the same and a split is flagged. Inside about 5 days, the local forecasters lead: the Japan Meteorological Agency, Weathernews and the Japan Weather Association in Japan, the Hong Kong Observatory in Hong Kong. The Global Forecasting Model becomes a cross-check only.
