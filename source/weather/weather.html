    How much rain is that

    Under 1 mmA trace. Spits at most.
    1 to 5 mmLight rain. Umbrella, carry on.
    5 to 15 mmSteady rain. Wet shoes, views gone.
    Over 15 mmHeavy. Move outdoor plans.

    How sure is each day

    Firm. Within 5 days. Plan on it.
    Fair. 6 to 10 days. The pattern is right, the timing of rain can shift a day.
    Outlook. 11 days or more. A trend from long-range models, not a forecast. Don't move anything yet.

    Which source wins: far out, every source counts the same and a split is flagged.
