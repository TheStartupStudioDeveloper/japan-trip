新宿

Shinjuku

East and west Shinjuku, Kabukichō, Golden Gai, Shinjuku-sanchōme, Nishi-Shinjuku and Shin-Ōkubo.

In the itinerary: Mon 19

Good to knowShinjuku Gyoen is closed on Mondays, so it only fits a swap day, and its gates close at 16:00 in October. Both Tocho decks are open on Mon 19. Fūunji and BenFiddich close on Sundays, fine for Monday. Takano Fruit Parlour hours are from 2022, so check on the day. Nothing here needs booking this week: book Kakiden or a New York Bar window seat only if you want them.

横浜

Yokohama and Kamakura

Minato Mirai, Chinatown, Yamashita Park, Motomachi and the Yamate Bluff, with Kamakura further out.

In the itinerary: Tue 20

Good to knowThe Cup Noodles Museum in Minato Mirai is closed on Tuesdays, so it is left out. The Manyo Club rooftop footbath is in the day plan as an idea: ask first, see How-to 22 in the itinerary in the itinerary. Weekday Chinatown kitchens close early: Jukeihanten last orders 20:00, Edosei shuts 19:30, which suits the 19:30 train. Manchinrō and Heichinrō have irregular closed days, so check the Tuesday when booking. In Kamakura the Buddha and Hase-dera close at 17:00, so a Kamakura swap needs to reach Kamakura by about 11:30.

京都

Kyoto

With Pontochō and Kiyamachi, Gion and Higashiyama, Arashiyama, Kyoto Station and Fushimi.

In the itinerary: Wed 21 Fri 23 Sat 24

Good to knowBook the Fri 23 Gion dinner this week: Endo Yasaka on TableCheck is the strongest pick, Nakatani is the cheaper kaiseki (closed Sundays only). Menami is closed Wednesdays, so it only fits if you swap the two dinners. Daiichi Asahi is closed Thursdays, no clash. Bee's Knees is closed Sundays, Kagizen Mondays, no clash. Jidai Matsuri, Kyoto's big costume parade, is on Thu 22 from about 12:00 (Imperial Palace to Heian Shrine), and the Kurama Fire Festival is that night: no clash while you are in Osaka, but if you swap Thursday and Friday, expect crowds and road closures in the centre. Hours for eX café, Yudofu Sagano, Sake Bar Yoramu, K36 and the temples were not all confirmed for 2026: check Google Maps the day before.
